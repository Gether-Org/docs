The Dashboard is the control center of Sledge. It provides a comprehensive, real-time view of your multi-cloud infrastructure status.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

At the top of the screen, four key metrics allow you to monitor the health of your operations:
* **Active Connections:** The number of cloud endpoints currently linked to Sledge.
* **Total Data Synced:** The total volume of data transferred (e.g., 4.2 TB), including a trend indicator compared to the previous month.
* **Migration Success Rate:** The overall success percentage of your active tasks (e.g., 99.8%).
* **Failed Sync Tasks:** The number of recent errors requiring your attention.

## Resource Management

* **Cloud Providers:** A summary table lists all your active connections (Provider, Name, Status, Region, Cost).
* **Recent Activity:** An audit log details the latest actions performed (e.g., "Assets Backup" to GCP, or "DB Migration" from Postgres to AWS RDS), ensuring full traceability.

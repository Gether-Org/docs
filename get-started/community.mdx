## Get help

The fastest way to get help is the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/kRPEZxR3V). When you ask a question, include:

- What you tried to do.
- What you expected to happen.
- What happened instead, with any error message.

## Report a bug

Open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Gether-Org/merops/issues) and describe the steps to reproduce the problem. Never include credentials or personal data in an issue.

<Note>
**Security issues:** do not report vulnerabilities publicly. Contact the team at [contact@merops.cloud](mailto:contact@merops.cloud) instead.
</Note>

## Contribute

Contributions are welcome, whether they are code, documentation, or feedback:

1. Fork the [repository](https://github.com/Gether-Org/merops) and create a branch for your change.
2. Follow the [local development guide](/get-started/local-development) to run the stack.
3. Open a pull request. The CI pipeline checks formatting, linting, builds, and tests automatically.

## Become a beta tester

Merops is in beta. To test new features before they are released and help shape the product, ask to join the beta program on [Discord](https://discord.gg/kRPEZxR3V).

**Merops** is a cloud resource migration and synchronization platform. It lets you connect several cloud providers, explore their storage, and move or synchronize data between them from a single interface.

<Tip>
**Change clouds, not architecture.** Merops helps you leave a provider without rebuilding your infrastructure from scratch.
</Tip>

## Key concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Provider" icon="cloud">
    A cloud account you connect to Merops, such as Amazon AWS or Google Cloud. You add a provider by importing its credentials file.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bucket" icon="bucket">
    A storage space on a provider. Merops lists your buckets and lets you preview the objects they contain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Migration" icon="paper-plane">
    A one-time transfer of objects from a source bucket to a destination bucket, possibly on another provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Synchronization" icon="rotate">
    A recurring job that keeps two buckets in sync at a defined interval, in one direction or both.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Supported providers

Merops currently ships integrations for **Amazon AWS** and **Google Cloud**. Integrations for **Azure**, **Scaleway**, and **Outscale** are on the [roadmap](/introduction/objectifs#roadmap).

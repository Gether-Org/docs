This guide walks you through your first migration with Merops: from account creation to a completed data transfer between two cloud providers.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- Credentials for at least **two cloud accounts** (for example, an AWS account and a Google Cloud account), or two buckets on the same provider.
- A credentials file for each account, in **JSON**, **YAML**, or **CSV** format.

<Warning>
Use credentials with the minimum permissions required: read access on the source bucket, and write access on the destination. Never share credentials with full administrator rights.
</Warning>

## Run your first migration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Open the Merops application and click **Sign up**. Register with your email and password, or continue with **Google** or **GitHub**.

    Once your account is created, Merops redirects you to your [Dashboard](/application/dashboard/accueil).

    See [Registration page](/application/register) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your source provider">
    In the sidebar, open **Providers** > **Manage**. Click the blue **+** button at the bottom right, then select **Import From File**.

    Choose the provider in the dropdown menu (for example, **Amazon AWS**) and drop your credentials file. Merops encrypts the file and adds a new node to your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your destination provider">
    Repeat the previous step with your destination account (for example, **Google Cloud**).

    Double-click a provider node to check that Merops can list its buckets and objects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the migration">
    In the sidebar, open **Bucket Migration** > **Start Migration**:

    1. Under **Source connection**, select your source provider and the bucket to migrate.
    2. Under **Destination connection**, select your destination provider and enter a bucket name.

    <Tip>
    If the destination bucket does not exist yet, Merops creates it automatically when the migration starts.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and launch">
    Check the file list in the right panel. It shows exactly which objects will be transferred.

    Click **Start Migration** to launch the transfer. Follow its progress from the **Recent Activity** section of your Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

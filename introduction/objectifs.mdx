## Our Value Proposition

Merops does more than just move files; we provide a comprehensive solution to the current challenges of cloud architecture:

* **Easy Synchronization:** Data transfer (e.g., AWS S3 to Azure Blob Storage) in real-time or on a defined schedule.
* **Tracking & Control:** A built-in monitoring tool to track transfers and configure custom criteria to control data flow.
* **GDPR Compliance & Sovereignty:** Facilitating migration to European clouds to ensure regulatory compliance.

## The Challenge of Digital Sovereignty

<Warning>
Today, **over 70% of European data** is controlled by non-European entities (such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud). Furthermore, 50% of these services' users are unaware of the US **Cloud Act**, which can compromise the privacy of their data.
</Warning>

**Merops's goal is societal:** We want to empower European companies to break free from Single-Cloud Dependency by drastically simplifying migration to European solutions and hosting providers (Scaleway, Outscale, etc.).

## Who is Merops for?

1.  **Independent cloud developers** looking to save time.
2.  **SMEs** (like legal SaaS companies) that handle sensitive data and require rapid migration without downtime and without advanced DevOps expertise.
3.  **Companies in sensitive sectors** (healthcare, defense, finance) requiring strict control over their data sovereignty.

## Roadmap

We have a clear vision for the product's future:
* **Sprint 1:** Integration of European providers (Scaleway, Outscale) and Azure, plus dashboard customization.
* **Sprint 2:** Advanced log management, synchronization failure handling, and pricing integration.
* **Sprint 3:** Support for new services (Lambdas, VPC) and the creation of the *Merops Academy* to streamline onboarding.

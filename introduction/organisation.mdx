## Our Tools and Methodology

To successfully deliver this ambitious project, we use a proven tech and tool stack:

* **Communication:** A structured **Discord** server (text channels for code, GitHub tracking, voice meeting rooms) for seamless daily collaboration.
* **Project & Code Management:** **GitHub** for versioning, CI/CD, and rigorous task tracking (Tickets *To Do*, *In Progress*, *Reviewing* separated by scope: frontend, backend, docs).
* **Documentation & Resources:** **Notion** for our meeting minutes, beta-tester database management, and tracking our success metrics (Greenlight).

## A Community Approach

We believe in the power of community. Sledge is being built with its users. We have already gathered a solid base of **Professional Beta-testers** from key companies (IBM, Outscale, AWS, Scaleway, Thales...) to ensure our tool meets true industry standards.

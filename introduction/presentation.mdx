## See the cloud differently

Today, the cloud is everywhere. However, managing multiple providers (Cloud Providers) or considering a multi-cloud strategy can quickly become an **absolute nightmare**. Technical complexity, high costs, lack of internal expertise... the obstacles are numerous.

**Sledge** was born from this observation. Our goal is to build an intuitive platform that makes it easy to synchronize and migrate data between servers hosted by different Cloud Providers, while offering a centralized view of the infrastructure.

## An interface designed for you

No more austere and complex interfaces. We designed Sledge with a **clean and intuitive UI** (Drag & Drop system) to let you orchestrate your migrations from a single place. Connect your sources, select your destinations, and Sledge takes care of the rest.
